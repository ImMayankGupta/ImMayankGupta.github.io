## Why should a Business Analyst understand Java?

Basic Java understanding benefits Business Analysts:

- Understanding the technical aspects of Java-based applications
- Communicating effectively with developers and technical stakeholders
- Analyzing and interpreting Java-based error logs and stack traces
- Assisting in identifying and prioritizing system requirements
- Bridging the gap between business and technical teams effectively

## Role of a Business Analyst in Java-based projects

- Gathering and documenting business requirements
- Translating requirements into technical specifications
- Collaborating with stakeholders on scope, objectives, success criteria
- Conducting feasibility studies and impact assessments
- Assisting in UI/UX design and validation
- Participating in User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
- Validating system behavior against business requirements

## Analyzing and documenting Java-based system requirements

- Collaborate with stakeholders to identify functional and non-functional requirements
- Create use cases, user stories, and acceptance criteria
- Document system interfaces, data flows, and process workflows
- Conduct gap analysis between existing capabilities and desired functionalities
- Work closely with developers to ensure accurate translation to technical specs

## Techniques to elicit requirements

- Interviews and workshops with business users, SMEs, technical teams
- Document analysis of existing system docs, user manuals, business processes
- Facilitated brainstorming and surveys
- Prototyping and wireframes to visualize interfaces
- Analyzing system logs, error messages, and performance reports

## Ensuring effective communication between business and technical teams

- Act as a bridge translating business requirements to technical specifications
- Facilitate meetings for collaboration and shared understanding
- Use visual aids, diagrams, and mockups
- Document and share project updates, progress reports
- Foster culture of open communication, active listening, and mutual respect

## Validating Java-based systems against business requirements

- Participate in User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
- Verify behavior against predefined acceptance criteria
- Conduct functional and regression testing
- Perform data validation for accuracy, integrity, completeness
- Collaborate with QA teams on test cases and coverage
- Analyze system logs, error messages, user feedback

## Handling change requests and scope changes

- Assess impact on project objectives, timelines, budgets
- Conduct impact analysis; communicate risks and benefits
- Collaborate with project managers, developers, stakeholders
- Prioritize and implement changes systematically
- Document change requests with updated requirements and specs
- Monitor and control scope creep
